Muse

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent — not just a chatbot, but an assistant that does work for you across your apps and the web. This page is a plain-language guide: what it is, what it can do, and where Muse users gather.

What is Muse?

Muse launched on September 8, 2026. A chatbot answers questions; Muse goes further — it carries out tasks. You describe what you want in plain language, and Muse works on it using a real browser and the apps you connect, and it keeps working after you close the app.

Every user gets their own Muse, running on its own dedicated computer. It remembers what you tell it, tracks your goals, and learns how you like things done. Muse is strictly personal: a conversation is always between one user and their own agent, and the app has no group or shared chats.

What can it do?

  • Research and browse. Search the web, read pages, compare options, and report back with sources.
  • Work across your apps. Connect services like email, calendars, drives, and social accounts, and Muse can read, draft, organize, and act in them.
  • Build things. Documents, spreadsheets, and web pages and apps (called Spaces) that live in your Library.
  • Remember and track. Long-term memory, a Goals tab for what you're working toward, and reminders and scheduled checks that run on their own.
  • Create media. Generate images, video, and audio, and publish a personalized Feed of short posts written for you.
  • Run errands. Compare prices, prepare purchases and bookings, and handle the legwork — stopping for your approval before anything is final.
  • Reach you where you are. Use Muse in the apps, on the web, or over WhatsApp.

The models: Muse Spark and Muse Wonder

Muse is powered by Meta's own Muse model family. Muse Spark (current version 1.3) runs the free plan. Muse Wonder, announced October 12, 2026, powers paid plans: it is Meta's most capable model, built for the long-running, multi-step work a personal agent handles every day — agentic tasks, coding, and personal and knowledge work — and it is fast to interact with.

Muse has a free plan with a usage limit, and paid monthly plans for more usage. Developers can also use Muse Wonder through the Meta Model API and Muse Code.

Getting Muse

  • Web: muse.ai
  • Phone: the Muse app on the iOS App Store and Google Play
  • Mac: download at ai.meta.com/muse/download
  • Availability: the United States, Canada, Japan, and Singapore

You stay in control

Muse is built to act, so the approvals matter. It confirms with you before consequential or irreversible actions — sending, buying, and sharing stop and wait for your go-ahead. Connected apps are permissioned one by one, and because your Muse runs on its own dedicated computer, your agent and your information stay yours.

Inside the app

  • Chat — the main conversation with your Muse, plus side chats for separate projects.
  • Spaces — web pages and apps Muse builds for you, kept in your Library or pinned in the sidebar.
  • Feed — short editorial posts your Muse writes for you on a schedule.
  • Ideas — suggestions for things Muse could build or do for you; pick one and it gets built.
  • Goals — the outcomes you're working toward, with plans and progress.
  • Library — where everything Muse makes for you collects: pages, documents, media, and files.

Community and learning

There is no official Meta-run Muse community yet, and Muse itself has no group chats. Users have started their own — all of these are unofficial:

  • Muse Agent on Discord — a community-run server (a few hundred members) for sharing Muse creations, workflows, apps, and connectors, and getting help.
  • Muse AI Community on Facebook — a Facebook group for Muse users; search Facebook for the exact name. A few thousand members.
  • awesome-muse — a community-maintained list of Muse guides, use cases, tools, and news on GitHub.

One caution that applies everywhere: your Muse is personal. Don't share your private information, files, or credentials in public communities.

FAQ

  • Is Muse free? There is a free plan with a usage limit; paid monthly plans give you more usage.
  • Who makes Muse? Meta. Muse launched on September 8, 2026.
  • Where can I use Muse? On the web at muse.ai, in the iOS and Android apps, and in the Mac app — available in the US, Canada, Japan, and Singapore.
  • What models power Muse? The free plan runs on Muse Spark; paid plans are powered by Muse Wonder.
  • Is there a Muse user community? No official one. Unofficial ones — the Muse Agent Discord and the Muse AI Community Facebook group — are listed above.
