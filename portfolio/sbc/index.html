About

Sweets By C is the home bakery of Caryesse Klapperich in Lake Crystal, Minnesota. Everything is homemade in small batches under a Minnesota cottage food license — real ingredients, baked fresh, and made to order.

What She Bakes

  • Custom cakes and cupcakes for birthdays, parties, and events
  • Cookies, bars, and pastries
  • Seasonal and holiday desserts
  • Special orders — just ask

How to Order

Everything is baked to order, so please order ahead. Call or text, or send an email with what you're dreaming up and the date you need it — pickup is in Lake Crystal.
